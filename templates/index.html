Grounded answers

AI Document Assistant

SmartFiles searches your documents and saved plagiarism reports first. Configure OLLAMA_MODEL to use your local Ollama model.

Ask a question about your files, research papers, or saved plagiarism reports. SmartFiles retrieves your own evidence and uses your configured local Ollama model to answer without inventing file contents or sources.

How it works

  • Answers use your local Ollama model and search only your own document passages.
  • Open a cited source to read the original file.
  • Ask follow-up questions or focus on one document.
  • Ask about saved plagiarism results to review verified passages and source links.
